# Placeholder scale audit

Status: **audit**, Phase 2A (2026-10-15). The target sizes come from the [world scale standard](WORLD_SCALE_STANDARD.md).

## Where placeholder sizes come from

Every placeholder data asset is generated by `Tools/Editor/setup_testmap.py` (`ue.ps1 -Task setup`).
- `fit_scale(mesh, L, W, H=None)` (`setup_testmap.py:325`) picks a **uniform** `MeshScale` that fits the mesh inside L × W cm. When H is given, it stretches Z to that height instead.
- Units are defined at `setup_testmap.py:1395-1426` and buildings at `:1427-1453`.
- `Tools/Editor/import_local_art.py` refits the Abrams to 290 × 160 when the local art is present (`:38`, `:109`).
- `make_building` sets `selection_radius = max(footprint) * 50` (`:432`).

The placeholders will be replaced (producer, 2026-10-15). The rescale is therefore **cheap and consistent**: new fit targets, nothing else. No placeholder mesh is edited.

**Effect on gameplay**
- Units are about 3.5× larger relative to today's distances. If ranges, speeds and maps did not change, every engagement would happen at bumper distance. Those values change per unit (next section).
- Group footprints grow about 12×, since footprint scales with the square of size. Ten tanks in formation went from about 12 × 10 m to about 40 × 30 m. This is the main reason for the [formation and avoidance work](RTS_MOVEMENT_PERFORMANCE.md).
- The harvester and the builder need wider exits and docks. The building exit clearance changes with them.

## Strategy summary

1. **Placeholders:** change only the fit targets, keeping a uniform scale. Fit infantry by height. Leave the M20 mesh alone.
2. **Per-unit gameplay values** (speed, turn, range, shell speed) move to the starting bands in one data pass in `setup_testmap.py`.
3. **Buildings and maps** grow together, in the same issue. Otherwise the bases do not fit the maps.
4. **Footprints** for collision, avoidance and formation are derived from the mesh, not hand-set (see [collision and selection](VEHICLE_COLLISION_AND_SELECTION.md)). That way a placeholder swap updates them automatically.
5. **Regression:** tests that hard-code distances need updating. The known ones:
   - the smoke tests' "within 8 m of the target";
   - `RTSAvoidanceTests`' 220 cm spacing;
   - building-test exit positions;
   - economy-test dock distances.
