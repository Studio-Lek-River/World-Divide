# Audio

Sound sources that `Tools/Editor/setup_testmap.py` imports into `/Game/Audio`. Each is recorded here with where it came
from and on what terms we use it.

| File | Used for | Source | Owner | Licence |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| `TankCannon/TankCannon_0{1,2,3}_{Blast,Body,Echo}.wav` | `SC_TankCannon`, the firing sound of `DA_TestCannon` and `DA_TestHeavyCannon` (the M20 Guardian's gun), #374 | Generated by the producer with Freeway (free plan), October 2026; delivered as `american_tank_{1,2,3}_{fire,body,echo}.wav` (IMA ADPCM) and decoded to 16-bit PCM unchanged otherwise | Lek River Studio | Our own work, subject to Freeway's terms of use for generated output |

Per take: a blast (about 1.2 s), a body (3–4 s) and an echo tail (3–4.6 s), 44.1 kHz mono. `BuildLayeredShotSound`
(`Source/WorldDivide/Core/RTSShotSoundBuilder.cpp`) mixes them by distance and plays one take at random per shot.
