# M20 Guardian prototype

Built from code by `Tools/Art/m20_guardian_blender.py` (Blender 5.1). Rebuild everything with
`blender -b --python Tools/Art/m20_guardian_blender.py`; check the FBX files with `... -- --verify`.

## Files

- `source/m20_guardian.blend`: the editable scene, with live Bevel and Weighted Normal modifiers.
- `fbx/SM_M20_*.fbx`: one mesh per moving part, each at its own pivot, in metres (Unreal reads it as cm), +X forward, Z up.
  The road wheel, sprocket and idler are each a single mesh that every axle uses.
- `m20_guardian.json`: pivots and sockets in Unreal cm (Y mirrored), in the same format as `abrams.json`, plus the
  gun, RWS, wheel and track offsets.
- `renders/m20_front34.png`, `m20_side.png`, `m20_top.png`, `m20_front.png`, `m20_pivot_test.png` (turret 45° left,
  gun 10° up, RWS 90° right). `stage1_*` are the blockout before detail.
- `report_stats.md` and `export_triangles.json`: generated by the script.

## Materials

Procedural Cycles node setups. They do not export to FBX: Unreal only gets the slot names.

- **Paint**: desert tan mottled by noise, darker in crevices (ambient occlusion), worn to bare steel on edges (bevel
  normal against the face normal, broken up by noise), fine bump.
- **Blue**: the same wear setup in USA blue, on its own slot so it can become the game's HouseColor slot.
- **Metal**: worn steel. **Rubber**: tyres. **Track**: dark steel. **Dark**: grilles, launchers, machine guns.
- **Optics**: blue glass with a faint glow. **LightWhite** / **LightRed**: emissive head and tail lights.

## Compared with the sheet (checked in the renders)

Matches: overall length, width and height. The layout: wedge glacis, long hull with bolted skirts, six road wheels
per side with a rear sprocket, front idler and three return rollers. An angular turret with chamfered cheeks, a rear
bustle with a rack and stowage, a gunner's sight front left, the commander's sight and 12.7 mm RWS on the right. Two
antennas. A sleeved 105 mm gun with fume extractor and muzzle ring. Blue ID panels on the mid-skirt band, the turret
flanks, the turret cheeks and the glacis corners.

Differs:

- **Turret clutter.** The sheet's turret is far busier, with boxes all over the sides and roof and a deeper bustle full
  of containers. Ours has fewer, larger forms.
- **Gun.** The sheet's barrel sticks out further, about 1.4 m beyond the hull front against our 1.1 m. With the 7.8 m
  overall length fixed, the sheet's own views do not agree on the hull length.
- **Silhouette.** The hull and turret still read close to the Abrams family. The sheet is like that too, but the
  identity rests on the blue panels, the slab armour and the RWS rather than on the outline.
- **Surface.** The surface is clean flat panels. The sheet's riveted plate texture, grime and chipped paint need
  textures, not geometry.

## Remaining issues before production quality

1. **No baked textures.** UVs are only a Smart UV Project, good enough for lightmaps but not laid out for texturing.
   The procedural materials stay in Blender. Production needs proper UVs, a trim sheet or texture set (base colour,
   normal, ORM), and the bolts and plate seams moved into normal maps.
2. **Bolts as geometry.** Several hundred bolt cylinders (20 triangles each) are real geometry. They should become normal-map detail with
   the high-poly baked down.
3. **No LODs and no collision.** At 47k triangles this is a close-zoom hero mesh. The RTS view needs LOD1 (about 15k)
   and LOD2 (about 5k), plus simple UCX collision.
4. **Rigid tracks.** Each track is a rigid mesh of individual links. Animating them needs a scrolling track material,
   or a spline or skeletal setup. The road wheels have no suspension travel.
5. **Static return rollers.** They are part of the hull and do not spin.
6. **Detail density.** The turret roof and sides need the extra boxes, cables, handles, lifting eyes, tie-downs and
   bustle containers seen on the sheet. The hull needs track-tension housings, fender details, a tool rack, the rear
   grille depth and an exhaust shroud.
7. **Shading.** Bevels are 1.5 cm, 2 segments, harden normals. The thin rack bars and launcher tubes are not bevelled.
   Some panel edges may need wider bevels for readability at RTS distance.
8. **Not in the game yet.** The follow-up step is a separate `DA_TestM20Guardian` unit.
